About this site

About Boise Mold Experts

Mold and water damage are stressful, and it’s hard to know who to trust when you’re standing in a wet basement. We make that part easier. You tell us what’s happening, and we handle exactly that kind of job across Boise, Meridian, Nampa, Eagle, Caldwell and Kuna.

What we do

Listen, then fix it

We pick up, hear what’s going on, and come out to handle that exact problem at your home.

Who does the work

We handle it

The inspection, cleanup and drying are done by us — Boise Mold Experts. We give you our own assessment and estimate.

What it costs you

Nothing to call us

Calling is free, with no obligation. Any inspection or job pricing comes from us, and we explain it before anything starts.

Why this site exists

Plain answers first

When there’s water on the floor or mold on the wall, most people search, call the first number they find and hope for the best. We built this site so you can understand what’s going on first, and then have someone friendly on the other end of the phone when you’re ready.

That’s why our pages are specific to local conditions: irrigation-season groundwater in Nampa, frozen pipes in older Boise homes, crawlspaces in Meridian, septic systems around Kuna. The information is general and educational, and it isn’t a substitute for someone looking at your home in person.

Before you hire anyone

Whoever ends up doing the work, we always suggest you:

  • Ask for a written estimate and scope before work starts
  • Ask what’s causing the moisture, and how it will be fixed
  • Ask about insurance and any certifications that matter to you
  • Get clearance or completion documentation at the end
