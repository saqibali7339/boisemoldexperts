Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information Boise Mold Experts (“we,” “us”) collects when you use boisemoldexperts.com or call the phone number listed on it, and how that information is used.

Information we collect

Phone calls. When you call, our call-handling provider receives standard call information such as your phone number, the time and length of the call, and your general location derived from your number. Calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Website use. Like most websites, our hosting and analytics tools may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited and referring site. We don’t collect your name or contact details through the website, because there are no forms on this site.

How we use it

  • To connect your call with an independent service provider in your area
  • To monitor call quality and improve the service
  • To understand how visitors use the site
  • To comply with legal obligations

Sharing

When your call is connected, the service provider you speak with will receive your phone number and whatever you share during the call. That provider’s handling of your information is governed by their own privacy practices. We may also share information with call-tracking and analytics vendors who process it on our behalf, and when required by law.

Cookies

The site may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics and to measure which pages lead to calls. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Your choices

You can choose not to call, or block cookies in your browser. To ask about information associated with your phone number, call us at (321) 985-3339.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
