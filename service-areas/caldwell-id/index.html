Caldwell conditions

What’s behind most Caldwell calls

Caldwell’s older neighborhoods around downtown and near the College of Idaho have many homes from the early and mid-1900s, with aging drain lines, older windows and crawlspaces that haven’t had a vapor barrier in decades.

Indian Creek runs through town, and irrigation canals and farm drains surround the city. Properties near them can see seasonal groundwater and occasional surface flooding during spring runoff or a ditch overflow.

Newer subdivisions to the east and south share Meridian’s profile: tighter houses, crawlspaces and appliance line failures rather than old pipes.

Questions

Common questions

Can a tenant call for a mold assessment?

Yes. Anyone can call us — we handle it. For repairs, tenants should coordinate with their landlord, who usually has to authorize work on the property.

Is mold always the landlord’s responsibility?

Not always. It usually depends on the cause, for example a roof leak versus moisture from lifestyle habits, and on the lease. Check Idaho landlord-tenant resources or talk to a legal aid service if there’s a dispute.

Do you serve all of Caldwell?

Yes, plus nearby Middleton, Notus and surrounding Canyon County.
