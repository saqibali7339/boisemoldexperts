Three kinds of ground

Find your Eagle setting

Zone A

River corridor

Where: south Eagle near the Boise River and its channels, Eagle Island area.

Ground: shallow groundwater, sandy and gravelly soils.

Watch for: basement and crawlspace dampness in spring high water and through irrigation season, and sump pumps that run more than you expect.

Zone B

Ranch & acreage lots

Where: north of State Street toward Floating Feather and beyond.

Ground: flatter land, often on wells and septic, with irrigation ditches.

Watch for: irrigation runoff toward the house, well-pressure plumbing failures, and septic-related backups.

Zone C

Foothills

Where: hillside neighborhoods north and northeast of town.

Ground: sloped lots, clay soils in places, walkout basements.

Watch for: water pushing against the uphill foundation wall, roof leaks on complex rooflines, and hidden plumbing leaks in large floor plans.

Larger homes

More house, more places for a leak to hide

Many Eagle homes are larger than the valley average, with more bathrooms, second-floor laundry rooms, wet bars, long plumbing runs and rooms that don’t get used every day. A slow leak in a guest bath or behind a bonus-room wall can run for weeks before anyone notices.

That makes leak detection the starting point in Eagle more often than elsewhere. A rising water bill, a meter that moves with everything off, or a faint stain in a ceiling below a bathroom are worth acting on early.

Questions

Common questions

Are homes near the Boise River in Eagle more likely to have mold?

They’re more likely to have groundwater-related dampness in basements and crawlspaces, especially in spring and summer. That moisture is what lets mold grow, so drainage and sump systems matter more there.

Does Eagle have a lot of homes on septic?

Many acreage and rural properties around Eagle use wells and septic systems. Others are on city sewer. If you’re not sure, check your property records or the bill you pay for wastewater.

Do you serve all of Eagle?

Yes, including the river corridor, north Eagle acreage, the foothills and nearby Star.
