Septic or sewer?

Two systems, two sets of answers

Most in-town subdivisions

City sewer

How to tell
Your utility bill includes a sewer charge, and there’s a cleanout near the house but no tank lids in the yard.
Who owns what
You’re typically responsible for the line from your house to the connection, called the service lateral. The city maintains the main.
Backup clues
Several drains back up at once, especially the lowest ones. If neighbors have it too, the main may be involved.
First calls
Stop water use, call the city’s public works line if you suspect the main, and get cleanup started.
Many rural & older lots

Private septic

How to tell
No sewer charge on your bill, tank lids or risers in the yard, and a drain field area.
Who owns what
You own the entire system: tank, pipes and drain field.
Backup clues
Slow drains everywhere, gurgling, soggy ground or odors over the drain field, or backup after heavy water use.
First calls
Stop water use, call a septic pumping service, and get cleanup started for anything inside.
Kuna conditions

Beyond backups

Kuna sits on higher, drier ground south of the Boise River, and many homes are newer. Mold calls here tend to come from inside the house rather than from groundwater: a failed supply line, a leaking water heater, an unvented bathroom, or construction moisture in a home closed up before it fully dried.

Indian Creek runs through town, and canals and laterals cross the surrounding farmland. Lots close to them can see seasonal dampness in crawlspaces.

Winters are cold and windy on the open ground around Kuna, so garage and crawlspace pipes are vulnerable to freezing.

Common questions

How do I find out if my Kuna home is on septic?

Check your utility bill for a sewer charge, look for tank lids or risers in the yard, or check your property records with Ada County. The seller’s disclosure from your purchase usually says too.

Do you help with septic pumping?

Septic pumping and repair are handled by septic contractors. We handle the inside of your home: extraction, removal of contaminated materials, disinfection and drying.

Do you serve rural Kuna?

Yes, including properties outside city limits and toward Melba and south Meridian.
