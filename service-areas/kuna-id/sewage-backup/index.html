Septic in Kuna

Keeping a septic system from backing up

  • Pump on a schedule. Your septic contractor can recommend an interval for your tank size and household.
  • Spread out heavy water use. Several loads of laundry in a row can overwhelm a tank and field.
  • Keep wipes, grease and chemicals out of drains.
  • Don’t drive or park on the drain field, and keep sprinklers from soaking it.
  • Know where your tank lids are before you need them.

Questions

Common questions

Who handles septic pumping in Kuna?

Licensed septic contractors pump and repair septic systems. We handle cleanup inside your home.

Is septic backup covered by insurance?

Coverage varies widely. Damage from a neglected or failing system is often excluded, while sudden failures may be treated differently. Check your policy.

How long does sewage cleanup take?

Small, contained backups can be cleaned in a day or two. Larger ones involving drywall and flooring removal, disinfection and drying usually take several days before rebuild.
