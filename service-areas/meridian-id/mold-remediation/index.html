Newer Meridian homes

Is it covered by the builder?

A large share of Meridian homes are less than twenty years old, and some are still within a builder’s warranty. Many builder warranties cover workmanship for a year and certain systems or structural items longer. Coverage for mold varies, and many exclude it unless it comes from a covered defect.

If your home is newer, it’s worth pulling the warranty documents before work starts, then:

  • Photograph the mold and anything that looks like the cause, like a leaking fitting, a missing vapor barrier or an unvented fan
  • Notify the builder in writing
  • Ask us for a written report on the cause

Even if the builder covers the fix, you may still want independent remediation to start quickly. Talk to the builder about how they handle that.

Questions

Common questions

Where does mold usually show up in Meridian homes?

Crawlspaces top the list, followed by bathrooms with weak ventilation and attics where bath fans vent into the space instead of outdoors.

Why is the actual job often bigger than what I can see?

Mold and moisture spread inside walls, under flooring and into insulation. Visible growth is usually the tip of the affected area.

How long will Meridian remediation take?

Most single-area jobs take one to five days including drying. Whole-crawlspace projects can take longer.
