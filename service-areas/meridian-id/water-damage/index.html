Why it spreads

Second-floor laundry, slab floors and open layouts

Many Meridian floor plans put the laundry upstairs, so a failed washer hose sends water through the subfloor into the ceiling below. Open layouts let water travel under laminate and engineered flooring across a whole level before it shows.

A burst supply line can release several gallons a minute. In an hour that’s hundreds of gallons, enough to soak flooring, the bottom of drywall and the crawlspace or ceiling below.

That’s why extraction followed by moisture mapping matters. We need to find where the water went, not just where it started.

Questions

Common questions

Can laminate or LVP floors be dried in place?

Sometimes. Some floating floors can be dried with specialized equipment if caught quickly, while others trap water underneath and need to be lifted. Moisture readings decide it.

Is a water leak in a newer home covered by the builder?

Possibly, if it’s from a workmanship defect within the warranty period. Appliance hose failures usually aren’t builder issues. Check your warranty and homeowner’s policy.

Do leak sensors actually help?

Yes. Inexpensive sensors under the washer, water heater and fridge can alert you to a leak in minutes. Some systems shut off the main automatically.
