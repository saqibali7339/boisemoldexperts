Older Nampa homes

Where remediation crews find mold in Nampa

The older neighborhoods around downtown Nampa, the Lakeview Park area and the north side have many homes built between the 1910s and 1970s. Common findings include mold on crawlspace joists where vapor barriers have worn away, around old aluminum windows, behind 1960s bathroom tile, and in attics where kitchen and bath fans were never vented outside.

Irrigation season adds groundwater to the mix. If your mold seems to get worse every summer, see the Nampa irrigation calendar. Timing the assessment while it’s damp gives the clearest picture.

Remediation follows the standard process: find and fix moisture, contain, remove affected materials, clean, dry and verify. In homes built before 1980, ask about asbestos and lead testing before demolition.

Questions

Common questions

Do swamp coolers cause mold?

They can contribute when pads aren’t replaced, the reservoir stays stagnant, or the house isn’t vented while the cooler runs. Maintained and vented properly, they’re usually fine in Nampa’s dry summer climate.

Should I switch to central air?

That’s a comfort and cost decision. Many Nampa homes run coolers without mold issues. If you keep one, replace pads yearly, drain and clean it each fall, and cover the duct for winter.

Can mold get into the ductwork?

Yes, especially in duct sections that stay damp. A specialist can inspect inside the ducts and recommend cleaning or replacement.
